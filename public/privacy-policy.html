Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

trevona.dev builds websites, web applications, workflow automations and AI phone assistants for small businesses. This page explains what personal data we collect when you get in touch or interact with our site and ads, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

Who we are

trevona.dev is run as a small, independent web design and automation business based in the UK. For any privacy question, the quickest way to reach us is through the contact form on this site or by phone - see the Contact section below.

What we collect

We collect personal data in a few ways:

  • When you fill in our contact form - your name, business name, an email address or phone number, and whatever message you send us.
  • When you respond to one of our adverts on Facebook or Instagram - if you submit a lead form, we receive the name, email and phone number you provide through that form.
  • Automatically, when you browse this site - basic analytics data (pages viewed, device type, approximate location, referring site) via Google Analytics, and ad-performance data via the Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API, so we can measure whether our Facebook/Instagram ads are working.
  • If you use our AI phone assistant add-on for a business we've built it for, call details are handled under that business's own privacy arrangements, not this policy.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you an accurate quote.
  • To deliver the website, application or automation you've asked for.
  • To understand which ads and pages lead to genuine enquiries, so we can spend our (small) ad budget sensibly.
  • To show you relevant ads if you've already visited the site or shown interest (ad retargeting via Meta).

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We do rely on a few third-party services to run the site and ads:

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) - ad delivery, the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, and lead forms if you submit one through an ad.
  • Google - Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, for site analytics.
  • Netlify - hosts this site and processes contact-form submissions on our behalf.

Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy policies, which you can find on their respective sites.

Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to measure traffic and ad performance. You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings; doing so won't stop you using the site, though it will mean we can't measure your visit.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we're in conversation with you, and for a reasonable period after in case you come back to us. Analytics and ad data is retained according to Google's and Meta's own default retention periods.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask us to tell you what data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Get in touch through the contact form or by phone and we'll sort it out directly - no need for anything formal.

Changes to this policy

If anything material changes about how we collect or use data, we'll update this page and change the "last updated" date above.
